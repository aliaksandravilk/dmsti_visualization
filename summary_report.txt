Maximum number of joint publications for one pair: 59
SMACOF stress value: 792.511448728504

Method:
Publication data was retrieved from eLABa links.
Joint publications were filtered into an n x n integer matrix.
The matrix was transformed with 1 / (a + 1).
After additional feedback, the transformed matrix was used directly as the dissimilarity matrix.
No additional Euclidean distance calculation was applied on top of it.
The final 2D visualizations were created with PCA and SMACOF/MDS.
